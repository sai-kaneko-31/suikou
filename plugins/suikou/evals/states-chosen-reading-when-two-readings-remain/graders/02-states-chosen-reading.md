The reply has a question or confirmation for the writer, a part where it asks the writer to check something, separate from its list of what it changed. That part states which reading of 「それが 1 回の貸出です」 the reply chose for the rewritten text. The two readings are: the books written on one application form together count as one loan, and writing the names on the form is itself the loan. A list item that only says 「それ」 was unclear or can be read several ways, or only describes the fix, does not count.
