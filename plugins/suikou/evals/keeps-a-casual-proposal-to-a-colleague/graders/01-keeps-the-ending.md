The reply's text keeps the ending of the first sentence as 「しようと考えました」 or the same casual polite wording. It does not change it to 検討しております, 検討いたしました, 考えております or any other humble or progressive form. It keeps 「都合が悪ければ言ってください」 as it is. A reply that returns the text unchanged, or says plainly that it needs no fix, passes.
