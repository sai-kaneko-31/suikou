The reply has a question or confirmation for the writer, a part where it asks the writer to check something, separate from its list of what it changed. That part states which reading of 「それが 1 件の申し込みです」 the reply chose for the rewritten text. The two readings are: the items written on one reception slip together count as one request, and writing the item names on the slip is itself the request. A list item that only says 「それ」 was unclear or can be read several ways, or only describes the fix, does not count.
