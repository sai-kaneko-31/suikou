The reply's rewritten text shows that 「月初に集計すると、数字が合いません」 follows from 「在庫は月末に確定する決まりです」. Any of these passes: joining the two sentences with ので, ため, ですから or a similar conjunctive form (for example 「決まりですので、月初に集計すると、数字が合いません」), or putting a connective such as そのため or したがって at the start of the second sentence. The premise (the month-end rule) stays first and the consequence (the numbers do not match) stays second. A text that still has the two sentences side by side with nothing joining them fails this check. A text that turns the relation around (for example saying the rule exists because the numbers do not match) also fails.
