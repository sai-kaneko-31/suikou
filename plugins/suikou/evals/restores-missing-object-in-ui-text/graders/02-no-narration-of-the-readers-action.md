The first sentence of the rewritten text explains what is displayed (that the list shows recipes that can be made from the selected ingredients), and does not merely tell the reader that they are looking at something. A sentence of the form 「ここに出ているのは〜だよ」, which explains what is displayed, passes; 「〜を見ているよ」 that only reports the act of looking fails.
