Judge only the rewritten text in the final reply. Ignore the explanation, the questions and the rest of the trace.

The two people are the administrator who invites and the person who is invited. Within the rewritten text, each of them is called by one noun throughout. These count as the same term: the same noun with a modifier (「組織の管理者」, 「招待した管理者」 and 「管理者」; 「招待された人」), and a reference to the invited person before the invitation is made (「他の人」, 「その人」). The criterion fails when the same person is called by two different nouns, for example 「招待された人」 in one sentence and 「参加者」 in another.
